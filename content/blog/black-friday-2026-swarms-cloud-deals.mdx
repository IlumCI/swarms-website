# Swarms Cloud Black Friday 2026: Every Deal and How to Claim It

Our Black Friday sale on [Swarms Cloud](https://cloud.swarms.world) is live now and runs **through Wednesday, December 2, 2026 (Pacific time)**. There are five offers, and most of them stack. Every one of them is listed on a single page, [cloud.swarms.world/black-friday](https://cloud.swarms.world/black-friday), with a button to claim it.

![The Swarms Cloud Black Friday page, live through December 2, with the free tokens offer for Pro and Premium](/black_friday_2026_deals_page.png)

Here is the short version:

| Deal | Who it's for | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free tokens | Pro and Premium subscribers | 100% off tokens on Black Friday (Nov 27) and Cyber Monday (Nov 30) |
| `SWARMSFLASH` | Every account, once | $15 in free credits |
| Half off three months | Anyone without a subscription | Pro at $10.00/mo or Premium at $50.00/mo for your first 3 months |
| Annual plans | New annual subscriptions | An extra 10% off your first year, on top of the standing 15% annual discount |
| Bonus credits | Anyone buying credits | 20% more credits, added free, on every top-up |

The sections below explain each deal and show exactly where to click.

## 1. $15 in free credits with SWARMSFLASH

Redeem the code **`SWARMSFLASH`** for **$15 in free credits**. That is enough to run your first agents and multi-agent swarms on any model in the catalog. Each account can redeem it once.

**How to claim it:**

1. Sign in, or [create an account](https://cloud.swarms.world/signup). New accounts also get the standard $5 sign-up credit, so a new account starts with $20.
2. Open [Settings](https://cloud.swarms.world/settings) and find the **Credits** card.
3. Type `SWARMSFLASH` into **Redeem a code** and click **Redeem**. The $15 is added to your free credits right away.

![The Redeem a code field in Settings with SWARMSFLASH entered, below the Buy credits options](/black_friday_2026_redeem_code.png)

## 2. Half off your first three months of Pro or Premium

If you are on the Free plan, start a monthly plan at **half price for your first three months**:

| Plan | Regular price | Black Friday price | You save |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | $19.99/mo | **$10.00/mo** | $29.97 over 3 months |
| Premium | $100.00/mo | **$50.00/mo** | $150.00 over 3 months |

There is no code to enter. The discount is applied automatically at checkout for any account without an active subscription, and you will see it on the Stripe checkout page before you pay. After three months the plan renews at its regular price, and you can cancel anytime from Settings. First-time Pro subscribers still get the 7-day free trial first, so the half-price months start after it.

**What upgrading unlocks:**

- **All 5 premium endpoints:** [graph workflows](https://cloud.swarms.world/workflow-builder), agent batch, swarm batch, [reasoning agents](https://cloud.swarms.world/reasoning-agents) and batched grid workflows.
- **Premium models**, including GPT-5.6, Claude Opus 5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro.
- **Higher rate limits** and the **priority compute lane**.
- **Night mode keeps working on top:** 50% off swarm tokens every night from 8 PM to 6 AM Pacific, which pairs well with the batch endpoints you just unlocked.

![The half-off offer on the Black Friday page, with Pro at $10.00/mo, Premium at $50.00/mo and everything the upgrade unlocks](/black_friday_2026_intro_offer.png)

**How to claim it:** on the [Black Friday page](https://cloud.swarms.world/black-friday), click **Pro at $10.00/mo** or **Premium at $50.00/mo**. You can also click **Upgrade to Pro** on the [pricing page](https://cloud.swarms.world/pricing) with **Monthly** selected. Either way you land on Stripe Checkout with the discount already applied.

## 3. An extra 10% off annual plans

Annual billing is now available on every Swarms Cloud plan, and it is always **15% off** paying monthly. During the sale, a new annual subscription gets **an extra 10% off its first year**, which comes to 24% off twelve monthly payments:

| Plan | Annual price | First year during the sale | Works out to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | $203.90/yr | **$183.51/yr** | $15.29/mo |
| Premium | $1,020.00/yr | **$918.00/yr** | $76.50/mo |

You pay once for the year, and the plan renews at the regular annual price. The extra 10% applies to new annual subscriptions. If you already pay monthly and want to move to annual billing, [contact support](https://cloud.swarms.world/support) and we will help.

![The annual offer on the Black Friday page: Pro at $183.51 for the first year and Premium at $918.00](/black_friday_2026_annual_offer.png)

**How to claim it:** click **Pro annual** or **Premium annual** on the [Black Friday page](https://cloud.swarms.world/black-friday). You can also go to the [pricing page](https://cloud.swarms.world/pricing), switch the toggle to **Annual, save 15%**, and click **Upgrade**. The extra 10% is applied at checkout. The same toggle is in [Settings](https://cloud.swarms.world/settings) under **Subscription**.

![The pricing page with the Annual, save 15% toggle selected, showing Pro at $203.90 a year and Premium at $1,020.00 a year](/black_friday_2026_annual_toggle.png)

## 4. 20% bonus credits on every top-up

Buy credits during the sale and we add **20% on top as free credits**:

| You pay | You get |
|---|---|
| $10 | $12 in credits |
| $25 | $30 in credits |
| $100 | $120 in credits |

The bonus applies to any amount, including a custom top-up, and to auto-recharges that run during the sale. It is added automatically once your payment goes through.

![The bonus credits offer: buy $10 and get $12, buy $25 and get $30, buy $100 and get $120](/black_friday_2026_bonus_credits.png)

**How to claim it:** in [Settings](https://cloud.swarms.world/settings), under **Credits**, pick **$10**, **$25** or **$100**, or enter your own amount and click **Buy**. Credits are worth $1 each and share one balance with [swarms.world](https://swarms.world).

## 5. Free tokens on Black Friday and Cyber Monday

On **Black Friday (Friday, November 27)** and **Cyber Monday (Monday, November 30)**, tokens are **100% off** for Pro and Premium subscribers. These are the days to queue up the large batch jobs, long reasoning runs and swarm experiments you have been putting off. Per-agent, image and search fees still apply.

The cheapest way to be ready is to combine this with deal 2 or 3: subscribe before the 27th and both free-token days are yours.

## Stacking the deals

- `SWARMSFLASH`, the bonus credits and the free tokens all combine with each other and with a plan.
- Each checkout uses one plan offer: half off three months if you choose monthly, or the extra 10% if you choose annual.
- For example, a new account that redeems `SWARMSFLASH` and buys $25 of credits starts with $50 to spend: the $5 sign-up credit, $15 from the code and $30 from the top-up. Add Pro at $10.00/mo and every premium endpoint is unlocked too.

## Frequently asked questions

**When do the deals end?**
At the end of Wednesday, December 2, 2026, Pacific time. The free-token offer runs only on November 27 and November 30.

**Do I need a code for the plan discounts?**
No. The half-off and annual discounts are applied automatically at checkout. The only code is `SWARMSFLASH`, for the $15 in credits.

**I already have a subscription. What can I use?**
`SWARMSFLASH`, the 20% bonus on credit purchases, and free tokens on November 27 and 30. The plan discounts are for starting a subscription.

**Where do I see what I paid?**
Every charge, credit purchase and redeemed code is listed on the [Billing history](https://cloud.swarms.world/billing-history) page, with Stripe receipts.
